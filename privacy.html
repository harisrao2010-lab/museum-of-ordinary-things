Privacy

Your collection
stays yours.

This note describes what this version of the Museum of Ordinary Things stores and which outside services it uses.

Your exhibits

The names, notes, room choices, and dates you enter are stored in local storage in your browser on this device. They are not sent to a Museum of Ordinary Things server. Use “Clear your museum” to remove them, or remove this site's local data in your browser settings. Clearing browser data or switching devices will also remove or leave behind the saved collection.

Typefaces

The site requests typefaces from Google Fonts. When those fonts load, your browser connects to Google's servers. If you block external requests, the page falls back to system fonts.

Ads and analytics

This version does not include advertising or analytics code. If those services are added in the future, this page must be updated to describe their data use and any choices visitors may have before they are enabled.

Contact and changes

This is a starter site's privacy notice, not legal advice. Before a public launch, its owner should add accurate contact information and review the notice for the laws that apply where the site and its visitors are located. This page will need an update whenever the site's data practices change.
