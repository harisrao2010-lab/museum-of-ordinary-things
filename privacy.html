Privacy

Your collection
stays yours.

This note describes what this version of the Museum of Ordinary Things stores and which outside services it uses.

Your exhibits

The names, notes, room choices, and dates you enter are stored in local storage in your browser on this device. They are not sent to a Museum of Ordinary Things server. Use “Clear your museum” to remove them, or remove this site's local data in your browser settings. Clearing browser data or switching devices will also remove or leave behind the saved collection. You can download a JSON backup and restore it on another device; keep backup files private because they contain the exhibit text you wrote.

Sharing is your choice

If you tap Share on an exhibit or the museum link, the site asks your browser to open its share sheet. You choose whether and where to send it. If the share sheet is unavailable, the site may offer to copy text and the public website link to your clipboard. Nothing is posted automatically.

Hosting and typefaces

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub says it logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. The page also requests typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers to retrieve them. The site falls back to system fonts if external font requests are blocked.

Ads and analytics

This version does not include advertising or analytics code. If those services are added in the future, this notice must be updated to describe their data use and any choices visitors may have before they are enabled.

Contact and changes

This is a starter site's privacy notice, not legal advice. Before a public launch, its owner should add accurate contact information and review the notice for the laws that apply where the site and its visitors are located. This page will need an update whenever the site's data practices change.
