Our idea

A museum for
what almost passes by.

We make museums for rare things. This little one is for the things that happen every day and somehow become precious later.

Small things count

A warm patch of sun, a familiar voice, a joke that lands at exactly the right time. These moments rarely ask to be remembered. The Museum of Ordinary Things is a gentle invitation to notice one and give it a name.

Your own private gallery

Write a short exhibit label and choose its room. Your collection is saved only in your browser on this device. It is not a public feed, and you do not need an account. We built it to make noticing easier, not to turn your day into another performance.

Keep the little things

There is no right size for a memory. If it mattered for a moment, it belongs here.
